# Executive Findings

The portfolio measurement design deliberately separates four questions that are often collapsed in executive reporting: inventory completeness, control effectiveness, exception operations, and business value. This prevents high adoption from being presented as evidence of good governance or realized value.

The remediation queue prioritizes initiatives with a combination of weak control coverage, many open exceptions, and long exception age. It should be used to assign owners and review cadence, not as an automated lifecycle decision.

The next implementation step would be a governed metric contract: lifecycle definitions, evidence links for each control, financial-accounting rules for realized value, and data-quality tests for source lineage and refresh timeliness.
